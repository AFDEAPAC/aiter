Two zones are not floors at all, and tables 3 and 4 leave them grey rather than colour a ratio that means nothing. degenerate is k ≥ N — the whole N=2K column at k=2048. There select_grid degenerates to a copy, reading every value and writing every index, while our kernel takes aiter's documented short-circuit and emits the identity with a -1 tail without ranking anything. We come out 2–4x "faster than the floor" because the two are not doing the same work. launch-bound is the vendored file's own warning, verbatim: an empty kernel costs about 6 us here, below roughly m=128 the floor IS that launch cost, and those cells tell you nothing about a selector. Our dispatch is cheaper than select_grid's — 1.48 us against 6.20 us at M=1 N=2K — so a ratio there measures the two harnesses' launch overhead, not the kernel.
